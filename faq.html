FAQ and Next Steps

Quick answers for beginners, and what to do after the ten lessons.

Is Blender really free?

Yes. Blender is free, open-source software from blender.org. This site is free too, with no sign-up.

Can my computer run Blender?

Blender lists its exact hardware needs on the official system requirements page. Check it before you download.

I do not have a middle mouse button. What do I do?

Open Edit > Preferences > Input and turn on Emulate 3 Button Mouse. After that you can hold Alt and use the left mouse button in place of the middle button, for example to orbit the view.

My keyboard has no number pad. Can I still follow the lessons?

Yes. In Edit > Preferences > Input turn on Emulate Numpad. The number keys on the top row then work like the number pad in the 3D Viewport.

How do I save my work?

Use File > Save. Blender saves a .blend file. Save a copy before you try something new, so you can go back to it.

Where do I find the lessons in order?

Start at the home page. Lessons 1 to 6 build a plant pot, and lessons 7 to 10 build a mug.

Where does the final image go after I render?

Press F12 to render. In the render window choose Image > Save As to save the picture. Lesson 6 shows each step.

Which Blender version should I use?

The steps and screenshots on this site are from Blender 4.5 LTS. Other versions can look a little different, so use 4.5 if you can.

Next steps

  1. Repeat the projects without the lessons. Rebuild the plant pot and the mug from memory, then check the lesson only where you get stuck.
  2. Do the practice challenges. The practice page has six short tasks with no single right answer.
  3. Learn the shortcuts. Keep the shortcuts table open while you work, and look up unfamiliar words in the glossary.
  4. Read the official manual. The Blender 4.5 LTS manual explains every tool and setting in detail.
