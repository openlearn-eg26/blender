About this site

This site teaches Blender to beginners, and more lessons will be added over time. The content is written from scratch, and the images are screenshots from Blender 4.5 LTS taken while testing the steps. You may see small differences in other Blender versions.

Version and sources

The lessons are written for Blender 4.5.17 LTS (free, open-source software). All the writing is original to this site, and the images are screenshots we took ourselves from the program. There is no text, image, music or file taken from other sites. Every step in the six lessons was tried by hand on this version, except Material Preview mode. If you use another version you may see small differences.
