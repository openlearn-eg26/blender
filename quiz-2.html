Quiz: Lessons 7 to 10

Twelve short questions on the mug project. Pick your answer in your head, then open the answer to check. Nothing is saved or sent.

1. What does a modifier do?

  1. Changes how an object looks or behaves without changing its mesh
  2. Renders the image
  3. Deletes the object
Show answer

A: Changes how an object looks or behaves without changing its mesh. A modifier can be switched off, changed or deleted at any time (Lesson 7).

2. Which Properties tab holds modifiers?

  1. The Output tab
  2. The Modifiers tab (blue wrench icon)
  3. The Material tab (sphere icon)
Show answer

B: The Modifiers tab (blue wrench icon). Click the blue wrench icon, then Add Modifier (Lesson 7).

3. In what order do modifiers run?

  1. All at random
  2. From bottom to top
  3. From top to bottom
Show answer

C: From top to bottom. Solidify runs first and Bevel works on its result (Lesson 7).

4. Why does Solidify do nothing on a closed cylinder at first?

  1. Solidify only works on cubes
  2. A closed cylinder has no open side to thicken
  3. The Bevel is switched on
Show answer

B: A closed cylinder has no open side to thicken. That is why Lesson 8 opens the top (Lessons 7 and 8).

5. How do you remove the top face of the cylinder in Lesson 8?

  1. Press Ctrl + B
  2. Press F12
  3. Tab, 3, click the top face, X, then Faces
Show answer

C: Tab, 3, click the top face, X, then Faces. Enter Edit Mode, use face mode, select the top face, press X and choose Faces, then Tab back to Object Mode (Lesson 8).

6. If the inside of the mug looks flat or dark, what should you check?

  1. The torus is deleted
  2. Shade Smooth is applied and the Solidify Offset is -1
  3. The camera is a light
Show answer

B: Shade Smooth is applied and the Solidify Offset is -1. Offset -1 grows the wall inward (Lesson 8).

7. In the Add Torus panel, what does the Minor Radius control?

  1. The size of the whole ring
  2. The camera angle
  3. The thickness of the tube
Show answer

C: The thickness of the tube. The major radius is the size of the ring, the minor radius is the thickness of the tube (Lesson 9).

8. What does Rotation X = 90 do to the handle torus?

  1. Stands the ring upright
  2. Makes it shiny
  3. Moves it to the floor
Show answer

A: Stands the ring upright. Rotation X = 90 stands the ring upright (Lesson 9).

9. The handle floats in the air. What do you check first?

  1. The file name
  2. Location X
  3. The render engine
Show answer

B: Location X. Location X = 1.05 puts one side of the ring inside the wall of the mug (Lesson 9).

10. How do both mug parts get the same material?

  1. Rename the camera
  2. Pick the same Ceramic material from the list in the Handle's Material tab
  3. Delete the Mug
Show answer

B: Pick the same Ceramic material from the list in the Handle's Material tab. Use the sphere icon next to New to choose the existing material (Lesson 10).

11. What does a lower Roughness value give you?

  1. A rougher surface
  2. A shinier, glazed look
  3. A bigger object
Show answer

B: A shinier, glazed look. The lesson uses Roughness 0.25 for the glazed ceramic look (Lesson 10).

12. What does a higher number of Samples in Cycles give you?

  1. A smaller file only
  2. A different camera
  3. A cleaner image, but a slower render
Show answer

C: A cleaner image, but a slower render. The lesson uses 32 Samples and says to use more for a cleaner image (Lesson 10).
