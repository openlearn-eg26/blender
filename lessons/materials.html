Lesson 4 of 10

Materials and Colors

Color the pot and understand Material and Roughness.

Select the pot (click it) and open the Material tab in Properties (the red and white sphere icon at the bottom of the tab column, just below the green triangle Data icon).

Steps

  1. If there is no material, click New. A new material called Material appears. Rename it to Terracotta by double-clicking the name.
  2. Under Surface you will find Principled BSDF, the main setting for most materials.
  3. Click the Base Color box and pick a terracotta color (orange leaning brown).
  4. Set Roughness to about 0.6.
SettingMeaning
Base ColorThe main color
MetallicFrom 0 (not metal) to 1 (fully metal)
RoughnessThe higher the value, the rougher and less shiny the surface
To preview the color on the object inside the Viewport, use Material Preview mode (the third sphere in the view buttons at the top right of the Viewport). On weaker computers it can be slow, and in that case you will see the real color in the final render (Lesson 6). The final image in Lesson 6 was made with a slightly lighter color than the one you will pick, so do not worry if yours comes out darker. We have not tested Material Preview mode ourselves.

Exercise

Try Metallic = 1 and Roughness = 0.2 and see the difference in the render later. Then go back to terracotta.
