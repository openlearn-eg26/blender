Lesson 8 of 8

Building the Mug Body

Open the top of the cylinder so Solidify turns it into a cup with walls.

We continue with the cylinder and the two modifiers from Lesson 7.

1. Delete the top face

  1. Press Tab to enter Edit Mode and 3 for face mode.
  2. Click the top face of the cylinder to select it.
  3. Press X and choose Faces.
  4. Press Tab to go back to Object Mode.

2. See the result

With the top open, Solidify gives the wall a thickness, and you can look inside a cup with rounded edges.

If the inside looks flat or dark, check that Shade Smooth is applied, and that the Solidify Offset is -1 (it grows the wall inward).

Exercise

Make a taller and thinner cup by scaling the object on Z only: press S, Z, 1.3, Enter.

The handle comes next, in a later lesson.
