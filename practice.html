Practice Challenges

Short tasks to practice what you learned. There is no single right answer for any of them.

1. Change the pot

Make the plant pot wider and shorter. Use Scale in Object Mode first, then try changing the Inset and Extrude values in Edit Mode.

Uses: Lessons 2-3

2. Three colors

Make three pots in three different colors and different Roughness values, then render them together.

Uses: Lessons 4-6

3. Thick and thin mug

Make two mugs, one with a very thin wall and one with a thick wall, by changing the Solidify Thickness.

Uses: Lessons 7-8

4. A bigger handle

Change the handle Minor Radius and Scale until the mug looks like a large coffee cup.

Uses: Lesson 9

5. Two lights

Add a second Area light with lower power on the other side and see how the shadows change in the render.

Uses: Lesson 10

6. Your own object

Make a simple object you know from your desk, such as a bowl or a cup holder, using only the tools from these lessons.

Uses: All lessons

Want a quick check first? Try the quiz on Lessons 1 to 6.
