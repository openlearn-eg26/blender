Blender Glossary

Short meanings of the words used in the lessons.

Bevel
Rounding the sharp edge of a mesh, as a tool (Ctrl + B) or as a modifier.
Cycles
Blender's slower render engine, with more realistic light.
EEVEE
Blender's faster render engine.
Edge
A line joining two vertices.
Edit Mode
The mode for changing the vertices, edges and faces of one object. Press Tab to enter or leave it.
Face
A flat surface bounded by edges.
Material
A set of settings that decides an object's color and surface look.
Mesh
An object made of vertices, edges and faces.
Modifier
A setting that changes an object without editing its mesh. Found in the Modifiers tab.
Object Mode
The mode for moving, rotating and scaling whole objects.
Outliner
The list of everything in the scene, at the top right.
Properties editor
The panel with tabs for render, output, object, modifiers, material and more.
Render
Making the final image from the scene, with F12.
Roughness
A material setting: higher values make the surface less shiny.
Solidify
A modifier that gives thickness to a surface.
Vertex
A single point of a mesh.
Viewport
The 3D window where you work on the scene.
