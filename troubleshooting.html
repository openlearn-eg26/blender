Troubleshooting

Common beginner problems and what to try first.

I cannot see my object

Press the Home key with the mouse over the Viewport to frame everything, or Numpad . to frame the selected object.

A shortcut does nothing

Shortcuts work on the window under the mouse. Move the mouse over the Viewport first. Also check that you are in the right mode (Object or Edit).

My numeric keys do nothing for Numpad shortcuts

A laptop may have no numpad. In Edit > Preferences > Input you can turn on Emulate Numpad, which lets the top row numbers act as the numpad.

The render is black

Check that there is a light and a camera in the scene, and that the light has some Power.

The render is very slow or grainy

Lower the resolution, use EEVEE, or reduce Samples. In Cycles more Samples means less grain but a longer render.

My changes are gone

Save often with Ctrl + S. In File > Recover you can try Auto Save or Last Session.

The modifier has no visible effect

Check the monitor icon in the modifier header, and that the object is selected. Solidify needs an open surface to show walls.
